🌟 2. Core STAR Stories Tailored for Payment Ops Risk

STAR 1: "Tell me about a time you built a reconciliation or control model to ensure customer funds and balances matched."
Focus: Bank balance vs. customer funds reconciliation, automated checks, 100% data integrity.
Situation At Santander UK, our analytics and financial reporting models processed over 1.2M daily transactions across multiple staging and target databases. In high-velocity environments, subtle timing differences or pipeline drops can create reconciliation discrepancies between source ledgers and reporting tables.
Task I was tasked with designing and implementing an automated, end-to-end reconciliation and control assurance framework to mathematically prove zero data loss and ensure 100% parity between source balances and downstream models.
Action I formulated an automated 3-Tier Reconciliation Engine using SQL and Python (PySpark):
  • Tier 1 (Volume Parity): Real-time row-count validation between source event logs and destination tables.
  • Tier 2 (Field Integrity via Cryptographic Checksums): Generated MD5/SHA256 hashes across critical columns (Account ID, Amount, Timestamp, Transaction Ref) to catch silent field truncation or rounding errors.
  • Tier 3 (Balance Control Totals): Reconciled absolute debit/credit sums and net position balances across ledger staging environments before any data was released to reporting models.
Result This engine eliminated 100% of pipeline discrepancy risks, automated what was previously a manual spot-checking process, and established a fully auditable data lineage that gave both internal risk partners and compliance auditors total confidence in our balance numbers.
STAR 2: "Describe a time when you identified an operational risk or anomaly that prevented financial loss."
Focus: Proactive investigation, SQL anomaly detection, £2.1M revenue/fee leakage recovery.
Situation During a review of portfolio transaction margins across digital banking accounts, leadership observed minor variance leakage. Routine reporting attributed it to general market fluctuations, but the root cause had not been isolated.
Task I initiated an in-depth data investigation across 1.2M+ historical transaction records to isolate structural anomalies in our fee calculation and payment routing logic.
Action Using advanced SQL (recursive CTEs and window partition functions) and Python, I segmented transaction data across account signup cohorts, product categories, and routing codes. I uncovered two distinct operational breakdowns:
  • An automated logic rule where promotional fee waivers failed to expire after the standard 90-day window on legacy accounts.
  • A merchant category routing mismatch that incorrectly classified high-volume commercial transactions under discounted retail interchange tiers.
I modeled the financial exposure, prepared a clear executive impact summary, and worked with engineering to deploy the corrective patch.
Result We recovered £2.1M in annualized financial leakage, corrected the routing logic permanently, and deployed an automated daily variance monitoring alert so any future misclassification is caught in real time.
STAR 3: "How do you approach designing Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) and risk dashboards for stakeholders?"
Focus: Looker/Tableau dashboards, KRI threshold alerts, self-serve enablement, 80% inquiry reduction.
Situation Our central analytics squad was frequently overwhelmed with repetitive ad-hoc inquiries from Operations, Risk Managers, and Product Leads asking for transaction failure trends, processing delay spikes, and daily break summaries.
Task My goal was to design a centralized Key Risk Indicator (KRI) dashboard suite in Looker and Tableau with automated risk threshold alerts, enabling stakeholders to monitor payment health independently.
Action
  • Held discovery sessions with Payment Operations and Risk Leads to define core KRIs: settlement failure rate, processing latency percentiles (P95/P99), break resolution time, and unallocated balance thresholds.
  • Modeled performant SQL and LookML views with pre-aggregated Persistent Derived Tables (PDTs) to ensure sub-second dashboard rendering.
  • Implemented automated conditional alerting: if any corridor or payment method exceeded its predefined risk appetite threshold (e.g. failure rate spike > 1.5%), an automated alert was triggered with root-cause drill-downs.
  • Ran training sessions for 40+ non-technical stakeholders to help them build their own custom slices.
Result Reduced repetitive ad-hoc operational inquiries by 80%, sped up root-cause investigation time during operational incidents from hours to minutes, and established a trusted single source of truth for payment risk.
STAR 4: "Tell me about a time you worked with auditors or risk partners to assure control effectiveness."
Focus: Collaboration with Audit/Risk, Technical Architecture & Interface Control Documents (TAD/ICD), data lineage.
Situation Prior to a major regulatory audit, internal risk partners and compliance auditors needed verifiable proof that our cloud data platform and financial reporting pipelines adhered to strict access governance and data lineage standards.
Task I was responsible for producing the technical documentation, control assurance evidence, and data lineage mapping to demonstrate the effectiveness of our internal controls.
Action
  • Authored comprehensive Technical Architecture Documents (TAD) and Interface Control Documents (ICD) clearly mapping data flow from core transactional systems to analytical warehouses.
  • Demonstrated our Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) in AWS Lake Formation, proving that sensitive customer PII was masked while audit teams had complete visibility into transaction history.
  • Conducted live walkthroughs with auditors, running automated reconciliation test scripts that validated mathematical checksums and control totals on demand.
Result Achieved 100% audit sign-off with zero high-priority findings, praised by risk partners for setting a benchmark in transparent, reproducible data governance.
STAR 5: "How do you prioritize your work when dealing with ambiguous problems and tight deadlines?"
Focus: Autonomy, structuring messy problems, cutting query latency by 45% under peak loads.
Situation At Santander UK, analytical query execution times on our Cloud Data Foundation were ballooning to over 45 minutes during peak month-end periods, threatening our ability to produce timely risk reports and resolve operational breaks quickly.
Task Without an explicit step-by-step roadmap, I took ownership of diagnosing the compute bottlenecks, prioritizing optimization tasks by business impact, and re-architecting the pipeline for speed and stability.
Action
  • Profiled the data pipelines and discovered two root causes: small-file fragmentation in S3 and unpartitioned, full-table scans in SQL queries.
  • Prioritized the highest-impact fixes: converted raw files into optimized columnar Parquet format, implemented multi-tiered date and category partitioning, and refactored SQL joins.
  • Scheduled non-urgent heavy transformations during off-peak windows and implemented query caching for repeated risk metrics.
Result Reduced analytical query latency by 45% (from ~45 mins down to ~12 mins), increased pipeline compute efficiency by 40%, and ensured critical risk reports were delivered ahead of SLA deadlines every single month.
